# EnderGram themes

EnderGram Noir is a dark theme with gunmetal surfaces and indigo accents;
EnderGram Frost is a cool white light theme. Both are colour palettes plus the
EnderGram chat wallpaper, shipped as standard `.tdesktop-theme` files:

`/usr/share/endergram/themes/EnderGram Noir.tdesktop-theme`
`/usr/share/endergram/themes/EnderGram Frost.tdesktop-theme`

Telegram themes change colours and background only; component geometry and
fonts stay part of the application.
